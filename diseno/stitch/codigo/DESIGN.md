## Brand & Style

Liz Pan blends the organic warmth of an artisanal French bakery with the high-reliability ergonomics of modern enterprise touch point-of-sale systems. Designed for tactile clarity on 24-inch touch terminals as well as tablet counters, the visual personality is calm, earthy, and organized under pressure.

The style combines **Organic Tactile Modernism** with clean corporate layout structures:
- **Warm, flour-and-linen surfaces** replace harsh sterile POS grays and hospital whites.
- **Botanical Sage greens** convey natural quality, freshness, and sustained operational focus.
- **Golden crust and baked amber accents** provide warm visual feedback for hot items, alerts, and transaction triggers.
- High-contrast, friendly rounded touch targets minimize operator fatigue and eliminate tapping errors during peak store hours.

## Colors

The palette establishes an earthy, reassuring operational rhythm:

- **Primary (`#2D5A43` / `sage-primary` & `#244936` / `sage-dark`):** Forest-grown botanical greens represent completed steps, primary navigation, active filters, and the final "IR A COBRAR" (Proceed to Checkout) action.
- **Secondary (`#D9822B` / `amber-crust` & `#E8973D` / `amber-warm`):** Warm crust and oven hues serve as visual highlights for fresh bakes, points/loyalty counters, and action arrows.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F7F5F0` / `warm-linen` & `#EFECE6` / `warm-oat`):** Warm paper/linen backdrops that cut screen glare in sunny bakery retail windows, framed with `#E2DDD3` (`border-subtle`) outlines.
- **Text & Contrast (`#1F2421` / `charcoal-dark` & `#545B56` / `charcoal-muted`):** Deep carbon typography ensures legibility at arm's length on high-brightness POS touchscreens.
- **Alert / Destructive (`#C03728` with `#FDEAE8`):** Restrained crimson reserved strictly for ticket cancellations, supervisor assistance, and negative quantity adjustments.

## Typography

The type hierarchy pairs **Space Grotesk** for numerical calculations, ticket IDs, clock counters, and bold total displays with **Plus Jakarta Sans** for product catalog readability, descriptions, and functional UI metadata.

- **Space Grotesk:** Technical geometric weight prevents numeric digit jitter when ticket amounts and quantities recalculate in real-time.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Highly humanist, open apertures maximize readability for cashiers reading catalog tiles at speed.
- **Uppercase Micro-Labels:** Used across cashier badges, status indicators, and keyboard shortcut pill prompts (`F1`, `F4`, `F8`) with tracking between `0.05em` and `0.1em`.

## Layout & Spacing

The terminal layout operates on an ergonomic **fixed touch rail + fluid dual-pane workspace**:

- **Navigation Rail:** A fixed 96px (`w-24`) left rail houses critical operational modes and emergency supervisor triggers within natural left thumb/hand reach.
- **Header:** Sticky 80px (`h-20`) top bar holding the active till station, synchronization pulse, real-time clock, and shift logout.
- **Main Catalog Workspace (8 Columns on desktop/touchscreen):** Responsive multi-column grid (`grid-cols-2` to `grid-cols-4`) housing oversized touch tiles with minimum 48px touch targets for instantaneous tap-to-add interactions.
- **Persistent Ticket Dock (4 Columns sticky):** Fixed checkout controller locked to the right side of the screen, ensuring the running subtotal, item quantities, and checkout buttons are always visible without page scrolling.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth follows a soft, non-fatiguing **tinted ambient layer model**:

- **Surface Level 0 (`#F7F5F0`):** The unadorned canvas foundation.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#FFFFFF` + `border-subtle`):** Catalog cards, search containers, and ticket summary docks. Outlined with 1px `#E2DDD3` and elevated by `0 3px 14px rgba(31, 36, 33, 0.05)` to separate white cards from warm linen canvas.
- **Floating Modals & Primary Triggers (`shadow-float`):** Keypad dialogs and interactive checkout triggers use a tinted botanical shadow `0 8px 30px rgba(45, 90, 67, 0.12)`.
- **Micro-elevation on Touch:** Tapping elements scales them down slightly (`active:scale-95` or `active:scale-[0.98]`) giving physical feedback akin to mechanical counter buttons.

## Shapes

The interface embraces generous, organic curves:

- **Large Surfaces & Panels:** `rounded-2xl` (16px) across cards, modal containers, and ticket modules.
- **Touch Buttons & Input Fields:** `rounded-xl` (12px) to provide defined, finger-friendly targets that prevent mis-taps.
- **Pills & Status Indicators:** `rounded-full` for categorical filters, loyalty counters, and live cashier state indicators.

## Components

### Buttons & Touch Triggers
- **Primary Checkout Button:** Minimum height 64px (`h-16`), solid `#244936` (`sage-dark`) background, bold Space Grotesk type, flanked with dual iconography and shortcut indicator (`F12`).
- **Tile Add Triggers:** 48x48px (`w-12 h-12`) rounded-xl green action buttons nestled at the base of catalog cards for effortless single-tap basket additions.
- **Cancel / Destructive Buttons:** Light strawberry background (`#FDEAE8`) with dark carmine text (`#C03728`) and border (`#F8C8C3`) to prevent accidental cashier clicks.

### Catalog Tiles (Product Cards)
- Composed of a warm sand thumbnail frame (`h-32`), bake timestamp pill badge, high-contrast title, packaging variant, and prominent price calculation in Space Grotesk.
- Border shifts on hover and selection from `#E2DDD3` to `#3C7356`.

### Category Ribbon Chips
- Horizontal scrolling pill ribbon with active state bathed in solid `sage-primary` (`#2D5A43`) with white text and category icon. Inactive chips maintain pure white backgrounds with subtle border outlines.

### Persistent Ticket Item Rows
- Compact layout containing item title, per-unit cost, inline stepper controller (`-` / `qty` / `+`) on `warm-oat` backing, calculated row sum, and discrete row-deletion icon.

### Micro-Modal (Touch Numeric Keypad)
- Dedicated 3x4 grid composed of 56px (`h-14`) high-contrast number keys (`0-9`), direct erase (`C`), and green confirmation (`CARGAR`) for manual scale/PLU and weighted items.
